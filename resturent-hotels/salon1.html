Our Story

More than a salon — a creative sanctuary.

Founded in 2008, Lumière was born from a simple belief: beauty is a form of self-expression, and every client deserves a stylist who truly listens. Our studio blends Parisian elegance with modern craft.

We invest in continuous education, premium products, and a culture of creativity — so you leave looking and feeling extraordinary.
